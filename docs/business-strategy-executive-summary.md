## 👥 Target Audience (4 Segments)

### Primary: Professional Musicians (60% of revenue)
- **Profile:** 25-45, indie artists, producers, digital-native
- **Pain Points:** Complex prompt engineering, language barriers
- **Willingness to Pay:** $15-49/month
- **Target:** 2,500 Poland (Year 1) → 25,000 global (Year 3)

### Secondary: Music Education (25% of revenue)
- **Market:** $1.5B → $7B by 2030 (25% CAGR)
- **Model:** B2B institutional licensing ($500-5,000/month)
- **Target:** 5-10 Poland schools (Year 1) → 30+ EU (Year 3)

### Tertiary: Music Therapy (10% of revenue)
- **Status:** Emerging segment with research validation
- **Pricing Premium:** 2-3x consumer pricing (healthcare SaaS)
- **Target:** 2-3 pilots (Year 1) → 5-10 clinics (Year 3)

### Adjacent: Content Creators (5% of revenue)
- **Volume:** High volume, lower price point ($5-10/month)
- **Strategy:** Bundle with Creator tier, upsell path
- **Target:** 500 Poland (Year 1) → 5,000 global (Year 3)
