## 2. Revenue Model & Pricing Strategy

### 2.1 Pricing Philosophy

**Value-Based Pricing:** Prompt quality = better music = higher value
**Tiered Feature Gating:** Not just usage limits, but capability differentiation
**Competitive Positioning:** Premium to Suno Pro ($10/month) but justified by specialization

---

### 2.2 Pricing Tiers

#### **FREE Tier (Acquisition & Data Collection)**

**Features:**
- 5 lyric-to-prompt conversions per day
- Basic prompt templates
- Community access (Discord, template library)
- Single platform export (Suno or Udio, choice locked)

**Limitations:**
- No advanced semantic analysis (mood/genre detection)
- No Polish language optimization
- No prompt history or refinement
- No batch processing
- Community support only

**Business Metrics:**
- Goal: User acquisition, model improvement data
- Conversion Target: 3-5% (industry benchmark: 2-5%)
- CAC Focus: Organic, content marketing, referrals

---

#### **CREATOR Tier - $19/month (Primary Revenue Driver)**

**Target Audience:** Independent musicians, content creators, hobbyists

**Features:**
- ✅ Unlimited conversions
- ✅ Advanced semantic analysis (mood, genre, structure detection)
- ✅ Polish language optimization (PLLuM-powered)
- ✅ Prompt history & refinement
- ✅ Multi-platform export (Suno, Udio, future platforms)
- ✅ 100+ prompt templates by genre
- ✅ Discord community access (Creator-only channels)
- ⚠️ No API access
- ⚠️ No batch processing

**Competitive Positioning:**
- 1.9x Suno Pro pricing ($10/month)
- Justified by: Polish support, semantic analysis, multi-platform
- Target ACV: $228/year

**Expected Mix:** 60% of paid users (Year 1), 50% (Year 3)

---

#### **PROFESSIONAL Tier - $49/month**

**Target Audience:** Professional producers, high-volume users, power users

**Features (Everything in Creator +):**
- ✅ Real-time prompt iteration feedback loop
- ✅ Custom style templates (save and reuse)
- ✅ API access (50 requests/month)
- ✅ Priority processing (faster generation)
- ✅ Batch processing (10 lyrics at once)
- ✅ Advanced analytics (prompt performance tracking)
- ✅ Email support (24-hour response)

**Competitive Positioning:**
- Premium tier for power users
- Comparable to DAW plugin pricing ($40-100/month)
- Target ACV: $588/year

**Expected Mix:** 30% of paid users (Year 1), 35% (Year 3)
